1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & CONCEPTUAL PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "Twist Deck" is a central gameplay mechanic in the AppForge hackathon.
Unlike conventional hackathons where participants build against a static problem
statement, AppForge introduces two unannounced, compulsory mid-event "twists"
that alter scope, constraints, or target audience.

The Twist Deck section is designed to:
  1. Communicate the rules and unpredictability of this mechanic.
  2. Differentiate between Round 1 ("Quick Pick" - agile feature addition) and
     Round 2 ("Heavy Pick" - major architectural or demographic pivot).
  3. Provide an engaging, tactile interactive experience where visitors can draw
     sample twist cards in real-time.
  4. Evoke a mysterious, premium "sealed challenge" atmosphere using atmospheric
     canvas animations, subtle card silhouettes, and gold/ink aesthetics.


2. ARCHITECTURE & COMPONENT COMPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The section is organized into three distinct layers:

[ Layer 0: Background Canvas ]  --> DriftingDeckBackground.tsx
     |-- HTML5 2D Canvas covering the full section bounds.
     |-- Ambient floating card silhouettes with physics, mouse repulsion,
         periodic autonomous draw locks, and amber glowing states.
     |-- Automatically throttled via IntersectionObserver.

[ Layer 1: Layout & Context Header ] --> TwistDeck.tsx
     |-- Section ID: #twistdeck with responsive padding (clamp(4rem, 10vw, 8rem)).
     |-- Section kicker ("The mechanic"), headline ("The Twist Deck"), and
         descriptive narrative defining rules (random draw, compulsory, no redrawn cards).

[ Layer 2: Three-Column Comparative & Interactive Grid ] --> TwistDeck.tsx
     |-- Left Column:   Round 1 Card ("Quick Pick" - light addition)
     |-- Center Column: The Interactive 3D Card Deck (Clickable / Keyboard navigable)
     |-- Right Column:  Round 2 Card ("Heavy Pick" - structural shift)
     |-- Bottom Caption: Subtitle clarifying preview status and event secrecy.


3. VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM & COLOR PALETTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The section leverages the AppForge custom color tokens defined in globals.css
and tailwind.config.ts:

  - Deep Ink Canvas (--ink: #07090d):
    Provides a pitch-black, high-contrast foundation for glowing gold accents.

  - Surfaces (--surface: #10141b, --surface-2: #171c24):
    Layered container colors providing depth. Round 1 uses a translucent
    surface (bg-surface/60), while Round 2 uses the denser elevated bg-surface2.

  - Gold Accents (--gold: #d9a94a, --gold-bright: #f4c862):
    Used for iconography, kickers, card borders, and amber canvas glows. Symbolizes
    high-stakes competition, sealed prestige, and primary action items.

  - Subtle Lines (--line: rgba(236, 232, 222, 0.10)):
    Ultra-subtle borders defining cards without heavy visual borders.

  - Typography (--paper: #ece8de, --muted: #828a97):
    - Headings & Kickers: Space Grotesk (geometric, modern, authoritative).
    - Body Text: Inter (high legibility, balanced neutral grey at #828a97).

   - Graphic / Icon:

5. THE INTERACTIVE 3D TWIST DECK (CENTER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The centerpiece is a clickable preview deck that lets users experience what a
twist draw looks and feels like.

A. Geometry & Sizing:
   - Container size: 176px x 240px on mobile (w-44 h-60), expanding to
     208px x 288px on desktop (sm:w-52 sm:h-72).
   - Golden card proportions matching real playing card ratios (~1:1.4).

B. Desktop 3D Flip Mechanics:
   - CSS 3D Scene (.deck-scene):
     * perspective: 1200px (creates realistic 3D depth and foreshortening).
   - Inner Rotator (.deck-inner):
     * transform-style: preserve-3d;
     * transition: transform 0.5s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.2, 0.2, 1);
   - Face Orientation (.deck-face, .deck-back):
     * backface-visibility: hidden; (prevents mirror-image leaking).
     * .deck-back has transform: rotateY(180deg).
   - State Toggle:
     * When flipped is true, .deck-flipped is appended, applying
       transform: rotateY(180deg) to .deck-inner.

C. Intelligent Multi-Click & Sequential Draw Timing:
   - If the user clicks while the card is face down:
     1. A non-repeating random twist is picked from the EXAMPLES array.
     2. The card flips to the back (rotateY 180deg).
   - If the user clicks while the card is face UP (to draw again):
     1. The card first flips back down to hidden face (setFlipped(false)).
     2. A 520ms setTimeout waits for the flip-down transition (500ms) to finish.
     3. Once hidden, the next twist is selected without repeating the current one.
     4. The card flips open again (setFlipped(true)), delivering a realistic
        "re-draw" experience without visual content jumps.

D. Card Face Design:
   - Front Face (DeckFront):
     * Inner gold outline inset by 8px (inset-2 border border-gold/25).
     * Gold tracked typography: "TWIST DECK" (tracking-[0.3em] text-sm).
     * Subtext: "Preview a draw".
   - Back Face (DeckBack):
     * Displays randomized twist prompt in high-contrast text-paper font-display.
     * Disclaimer caption: "Example only — real twists are sealed until the event."

E. Example Twist Pool:
   1. "your app must now work fully offline."
   2. "a new user group just joined — teenagers, not adults."
   3. "the whole UI must now pass a strict dark-mode-only constraint."
   4. "your app has to work with a 2G connection — under 200 KB per screen."
   5. "add real-time collaboration for at least two simultaneous users."


6. ACCESSIBILITY & GRACEFUL DEGRADATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Mobile & Touch Screen Adaptation:
   - Touch devices often stutter or exhibit clipping artifacts with nested CSS
     perspective and preserve-3d transforms.
   - Detected via media query: window.matchMedia("(hover: none)").matches.
   - On touch screens, the component automatically enters "fadeMode":
     * Bypasses 3D rotation entirely.
     * Uses a smooth crossfade between front and back faces (opacity transition
       400ms with a 200ms stagger).

B. Reduced Motion (prefers-reduced-motion):
   - Detected via window.matchMedia("(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)").matches.
   - Disables 3D rotational animations.
   - Switches central deck to instant/fade crossfade.
   - Disables continuous motion and particle tumbling in the canvas background.

C. Keyboard & Assistive Technology (A11y):
   - Accessible interactive element: Uses a native <button type="button">.
   - Has clear aria-label="Preview a draw from the example twist deck".
   - Handles onKeyDown for Space and Enter keys.
   - Front and back faces dynamically manage aria-hidden attributes based on
     flip state to prevent screen-reader confusion.


7. AMBIENT BACKGROUND SIMULATION (DriftingDeckBackground.tsx)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Behind the UI sits a custom canvas-based particle system designed specifically
to mimic cards tumbling through deep space:

A. Simulation Parameters:
   - Card Count: 16 cards on desktop, 8 cards on mobile screens (< 640px).
   - Card Dimensions: 70px x 98px base silhouette with 8px border-radius.
   - Depth Illusion: Each card receives a random scale factor (0.6x to 1.2x)
     and opacity (0.12 to 0.22) simulating a 3D z-plane.
   - Pseudo-3D Y-Axis Flip:
     * Calculated via flipScale = Math.abs(Math.cos(card.flipPhase)).
     * Compresses card width horizontally, simulating a continuous tumbling card
       in 2D canvas without the overhead of a full WebGL context.

B. Interactive Cursor Repulsion (Desktop Only):
   - Repulsion radius: 140px.
   - Repulsion strength: 0.8.
   - Cards smoothly steer away when the visitor's cursor approaches, giving the
     feeling of disturbing a deck floating in zero-gravity.

C. Autonomous "Card Draw" Event State Machine:
   - At random intervals (between 8,000ms and 15,000ms), an idle floating card is
     selected by the background system to simulate a live draw:
     1. "locking" phase (400ms): The card decelerates rapidly (vx/vy * 0.92),
        rotation dampens, and stroke opacity increases.
     2. "pulsing" phase (700ms): The card stops moving entirely, flashes with
        an amber glow (shadowColor: #d9a94a, blur: 18px), and reveals an inner
        wireframe border.
     3. "fading" phase (500ms): The card slowly dissolves out.
     4. Respawn: Re-initializes as a new random card at canvas boundary.

D. Performance & Efficiency Safeguards:
   - Retina DPR scaling: Automatically scales to window.devicePixelRatio (capped at 2x).
   - IntersectionObserver: Automatically stops the requestAnimationFrame loop
     when the section is not visible on screen, consuming 0% GPU/CPU while
     the user browses other sections.
   - pointer-events: none ensures zero scroll or touch event interference.


8. SUMMARY OF DESIGN IMPACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Twist Deck section balances informative hackathon rule communication with
gamified tactile interaction. By combining:
  1. Clear visual hierarchy (Light Round 1 vs. Heavy Round 2).
  2. Physical interaction (3D flipping card deck with realistic delay timing).
  3. Cinematic atmosphere (zero-overhead ambient canvas with mouse physics).
  4. Robust fallback engineering (touch detection, reduced-motion, a11y keyboarding).

It establishes one of the most memorable, polished, and thematic sections of the
AppForge landing experience.
